- id: c01
  title: Sahil Lavingia 的 Gumroad 失败与重塑 (VC 陷阱与可持续盈利)
  type: case
  source_chapter: 引言 + 第1章 The Minimalist Entrepreneur
  source_quote: |
    "Gumroad's pitched flight into the stratosphere leveled off after we burned through about $10 million in venture capital. After nine months of trying to raise more funding, we failed. In October 2015, I laid off three-quarters of the staff--including many good friends."
  summary: |
    19 岁的 Sahil 离开 Pinterest (员工 #2)，建 Gumroad 一个周末，融资 $8M (Accel、Kleiner Perkins、Max Levchin 等顶级 VC)。烧光约 $10M 后增长失速，融资失败，被迫裁员 75%。然后他搬去 Provo Utah，从零开始重构 Gumroad 为一家盈利优先的小公司。
  bound_to:
    - "VC 陷阱 / 不应盲目追逐独角兽"
    - "盈利优先 (profitability first)"
    - "心态转变: 从估值到可持续"
  outcome: |
    Gumroad 转型为低运营成本、按月微利运营的精品公司 (作者称之为 glorified Sahil-cloning factory)，不再追逐 billion-dollar 退出。
  tags: [case, gumroad, sahil, vc-trap, profitability-first, chapter-1]

- id: c02
  title: Peter Askew / VidaliaOnions.com (先社区 + 单一产品 + 长期稳健)
  type: case
  source_chapter: 第1章 The Minimalist Entrepreneur
  source_quote: |
    "Rather than trying to make a quick buck, he would build real businesses around domain names, asking himself with each one, 'Do I feel inspired? Is there a real business here?,' and, most of all, 'Can I be helpful?'"
  summary: |
    亚特兰大程序员 Peter Askew (6尺8) 失业后转向购买过期域名建"真正的生意"。他以 $14,000 拍下 VidaliaOnions.com (佐治亚州特产甜洋葱)，自己负责营销/客服/物流，把包装外包给当地农场 Aries Haygood。拒绝了 VC 路径 "扩张—融资—再扩张" 的诱惑，专注于单点包装棚能覆盖的时效配送市场。
  bound_to:
    - "从社区出发 / 你是社区一员"
    - "右尺寸市场 (Goldilocks size)"
    - "说 no 到 VC / 盈利优先"
    - "卖具体解决方案, 不卖平台"
  outcome: |
    六年时间把一个实验性副业做成全功能生意; 没有扩张到伦敦/东京/悉尼; 上一任东家因想扩张 (卖调味品/酱料) 而破产。
  tags: [case, peter-askew, vidalia, community-first, right-size, profitability, chapter-1]

  type: case
  source_chapter: 第2章 Start with Community

- id: c06
  title: Derek Sivers / CD Baby (手工先做, 再产品化)
  type: case
  source_chapter: 第3章 Build as Little as Possible
  source_quote: |
    "If you want to make a movie recommendation service, start by telling friends to call you for movie recommendations. When you find a movie your friends like, they buy you a drink. Keep track of what you recommended and how your friends liked it, and improve from there."

  bound_to:
    - "Manual Valuable Process (MVP) 先于产品"
    - "解决一个客户再扩张"
  outcome: |
    CD Baby 成为独立音乐发行领军, 后被 Disc Makers 收购。
  tags: [case, derek-sivers, cd-baby, manual-first, mvp, chapter-3]
